Responsible curriculum development

Use old books as references—not as copy.

We can turn your legally usable source material into a modern course structure while writing new explanations, examples, and practice problems.

Materials we can safely build from

  • Books and scans you own and have permission to adapt
  • Public-domain textbooks
  • Openly licensed educational resources
  • Your notes, lesson plans, and original teaching materials
  • AP course frameworks used as curricular guidance

Copyrighted textbooks should not be copied, republished, or closely paraphrased without permission. Final course content should be original and independently reviewed.
